3. Hotel and resort spa partners, Facebook

5. Regional Ontario spa partners, Facebook

English Canadian and French Canadian ads live inside every campaign. Regional ad sets cover Toronto, the Greater Toronto Area, Niagara, Ottawa, Muskoka and Hamilton. Retargeting excludes completed Ontario lead submissions.

## Naming rule

Every campaign, ad set and ad name begins with:

`ONTARIO | ADIR MADE, DO NOT DELETE`

## Business qualification

Target established commercial spa and wellness venues, with priority for hotel spas, resort spas, day spas, Nordic spas and destination wellness venues.

Do not target hair salons, medical spas, clinics, home-based businesses, mobile therapists or solo treatment rooms.

## Media integrity

The Ontario hero is an illustrative launch concept and must never be presented as an actual Ontario spa.

The Quebec venue images are approved examples already used by SpaPlus Canada. Creative that uses them explicitly says SpaPlus is live in Quebec and Ontario is next.
