Before you apply

Important questions, clear answers.

What is a SpaPlus country partnership?

It is an operating business partnership to build SpaPlus in a local market. SpaPlus provides the brand, technology, systems and know-how. The local partner builds the provider network and leads the local operation.

Is this an investment offer?

No. This page is for exploring an operating partnership. It is not an investment offer, fundraising process or promise of returns.

Is exclusivity included?

Not automatically. Every market, territory and operating condition is reviewed separately and exists only if included in a signed agreement.

What does SpaPlus provide?

Depending on the agreement, SpaPlus may provide the brand, local website, booking journey, business systems, automation, training, product support and marketing tools.

What does the local partner lead?

The local partner leads spa recruitment, sales, service, marketing, staffing and local operations, including compliance with local law as agreed.

What happens after I submit my details?

The team reviews the person and the market. If there is a strong initial fit, SpaPlus will contact you for an introductory conversation and the next stage of information.
