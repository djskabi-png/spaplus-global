# SpaPlus Ontario launch playbook

Status: Published for organic discovery and campaign landing traffic. No advertising spend is authorized by publication alone.

## Language and regional campaign architecture

The launch funnel is available in Canadian English and Canadian French.

- Ontario, English: `/en-ca/ontario/`
- Ontario, French: `/fr-ca/ontario/`
- Regional English pages: `/en-ca/ontario/{area}/`
- Regional French pages: `/fr-ca/ontario/{area}/`

Prepared campaign areas:

- Toronto
- Greater Toronto Area
- Niagara
- Ottawa
- Muskoka
- Hamilton

Each regional page has a dedicated URL, local positioning, regional focus
points, language alternate links and a form payload that records the selected
area and language. Regional pages are public and indexable after the production
release approved by Adir.

Do not create thin city pages only to increase page count. A new regional page
must have a verified campaign purpose, local positioning, a distinct audience
or offer and a working lead path.

## Objective

Build a qualified founding group of established Ontario spa businesses before
SpaPlus opens consumer booking inventory in the province.

The first focus is Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. The page remains valid
for established spas elsewhere in Ontario.

## Truth boundary

- SpaPlus is live in Québec.
- SpaPlus is not yet live in Ontario.
- Ontario launch timing has not been announced.
- Registration is an expression of interest.
- Registration has no fee, no commitment and no credit card requirement.
- Founding partner terms are not guaranteed.
- A commercial offer is shared separately in writing after a fit review.

## End-to-end funnel

1. A spa owner or manager sees an Ontario campaign ad or organic result.
2. The visitor lands on `/en-ca/ontario/` with campaign parameters preserved.
3. The page explains the model, qualification criteria and launch status.
4. The visitor starts the form.
5. The visitor submits business and contact details with privacy consent.
6. The submission is archived in the SpaPlus database.
7. The SpaPlus team receives a branded lead email.
8. The spa receives an immediate branded confirmation email.
9. The lead is reviewed within 72 hours.
10. A qualified lead is contacted using its selected channel.
11. A short discovery call confirms fit, decision-making authority and needs.
12. A written offer is shared only when both sides want to continue.
13. An approved spa moves into onboarding and launch preparation.

## Qualification

A strong lead normally has:

- A physical commercial spa or wellness venue in Ontario
- A public website or active business profile
- Bookable guest experiences
- A decision-maker or authorized manager as the contact
- Clear service categories and operating location
- Interest in new guest acquisition and online booking

Do not onboard through this campaign:

- Individual mobile therapists
- Home-based services
- Solo private treatment rooms
- Businesses outside Ontario
- Enquiries without a verifiable business presence

## Lead handling

Use the existing submission states:

- `new`: not reviewed
- `in_progress`: reviewed, contacted or in discussion
- `closed`: declined, unresponsive or completed outside the current workflow

Recommended internal service level:

- Automatic confirmation: immediately after a successful submission
- First review: within 24 hours
- First human response: no later than 72 hours
- Second follow-up: three business days after the first contact
- Final follow-up: seven business days after the second contact

Never promise commercial terms or launch dates in an automatic message.

## Paid campaign structure

Campaign one: Founding spa partners

- Objective: qualified lead submissions
- Geography: Ontario, with separate reporting for Toronto and the Greater
  Toronto Area
- Destination: the Ontario early-access page
- Exclusions: existing leads and irrelevant consumer audiences when available

Ad set one:

- Spa owners, founders and general managers

Ad set two:

- Hotel, resort and hospitality spa decision-makers

Ad set three:

- Retargeting for page visitors who did not submit

Do not mix consumer spa-booking ads into this business acquisition campaign.

## Organic launch

The Ontario page and its regional variants are public and indexable. Any future
market should remain private until its content, media and release gate pass.

### Search and answer-engine readiness

- Unique English and Canadian French titles and descriptions for Ontario and
  every regional campaign page.
- Canonical URLs, reciprocal language alternates and an English default.
- Geographic metadata for Ontario and the selected campaign area.
- Organization, website, webpage, breadcrumb, area-list and FAQ structured
  data without claiming a live Ontario branch.
- Ontario routes in the XML sitemap.
- A factual `llms.txt` summary for answer engines, including launch and media
  accuracy notes.
- Campaign attribution for standard UTM parameters, Google click IDs and Meta
  click IDs.

After approval:

1. Change the page to index and follow.
2. Add the canonical page to the sitemap.
3. Link to the page from the Canada and global country sections.
4. Add an English Canadian language alternate.
5. Submit the page through the connected search console.
6. Publish supporting articles only after factual review.

Suggested supporting topics:

- How SpaPlus plans to work with Ontario spa partners
- What Ontario spas should prepare before joining a booking platform
- Spa package ideas for couples, groups and individual guests
- Online booking and availability management for spa businesses

Do not target consumer booking claims until Ontario inventory is live.

## Launch gates

The page can be deployed after a single explicit approval, but lead acquisition
and commercial onboarding need these verified inputs:

- Confirmed internal recipient email list
- Approved person responsible for the 72-hour response
- Approved written founding partner offer
- Approved commercial and commission framework
- Meta advertising account access
- Campaign budget and billing approval
- Final privacy and commercial review
- Analytics and advertising consent configuration

No external ad, outreach message, offer or contract is published without the
corresponding approval and verified account access.
